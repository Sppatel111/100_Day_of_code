14. Select the name of the employee who is department head of HR.


select empname from empdetails where empid=any(select depthead from empdept where deptname='HR');
select e.empname from empdept as d inner join empdetails as e on d.depthead == e.empid where deptname='HR';

16. Select the name and email of the Dept Head who is not Permanent.
